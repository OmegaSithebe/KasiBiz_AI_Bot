Basic Business Budgeting

A budget helps you understand:

- Money coming in
- Money going out
- Expected profit
- Expected expenses

Track:

1. Sales
2. Stock purchases
3. Rent
4. Electricity
5. Transport
6. Employee wages
7. Airtime and data
