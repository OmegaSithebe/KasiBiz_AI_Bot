CIPC Overview

CIPC stands for the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission.

CIPC is responsible for maintaining company records and business registrations in South Africa.

CIPC obligations are separate from SARS obligations.

A business may have responsibilities to both CIPC and SARS.

Common CIPC responsibilities include:

- Company registration
- Annual returns
- Beneficial ownership declarations
- Director changes
- Address updates
- Company deregistration
- Company record maintenance

Businesses should keep their information updated with CIPC to remain compliant.
