Q: What is CIPC?
A: CIPC is the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission.

Q: Do I need to register my business?
A: Requirements depend on the business structure and circumstances.

Q: What is an annual return?
A: An annual return is a compliance submission used to confirm company information.

Q: What is beneficial ownership?
A: Beneficial ownership refers to the natural persons who ultimately own or control a company.

Q: Can a dormant company ignore annual returns?
A: No. Registered entities may still have compliance obligations.

Q: Why should company records be updated?
A: Accurate records help maintain compliance and support future business activities.
