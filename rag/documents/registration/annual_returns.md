CIPC Annual Returns

Every company and close corporation registered with CIPC is required to submit annual returns.

The requirement applies even if the company is not actively trading.

Annual returns help confirm:

- Registered address
- Director information
- Ownership information
- Company details
- Turnover category

Annual returns must be submitted within the filing period required by CIPC.

Failure to submit annual returns can result in:

- Penalties
- Compliance issues
- Deregistration risk

Businesses should track annual return deadlines carefully.
